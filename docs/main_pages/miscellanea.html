Books: what I’m reading, and what I’ve read since 2023.

I read in English, Italian and Spanish (and, slowly, other languages). I have a soft spot for Russian short stories, which I am happy to read in translation, and I am part of a book club, currently working through Raymond Chandler. This list is a great place to find your next read (I also like my own analysis of it), and I have used Audible a lot.
